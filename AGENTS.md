# Glosso project guide

This is the source for glosso.org. Build and maintain it as a contemporary independent art and literary magazine.

## Absolute editorial policy

- All published artistic and editorial prose must be human-authored. Codex must not generate, rewrite, paraphrase, summarize, embellish or invent it.
- This includes homepage introductions and slogans, definitions, philosophy, manifestos, About, Submissions and Contact copy, magazine introductions, article titles, excerpts and bodies, contributor biographies, promotional text and calls to action.
- Use the exact visible placeholder `BLANK` wherever required editorial prose has not been personally supplied and approved by the owner. Do not use lorem ipsum or invented sample work.
- Preserve owner-identified human-authored content verbatim, including unconventional capitalization, punctuation, line breaks, spacing and typographic composition. Instructions supplied as design context are not automatically approved website copy.
- Functional navigation labels and necessary accessible interface text are permitted. Proper names supplied by the owner (Glosso, Glosso Collective, Glosso Magazine) may be used as labels.
- The owner supplied the homepage recruitment notice and then requested UI rather than UX: "Human UI Designer Needed! Want UI Experience? Be a part of Glosso." Preserve this wording in UXNotice. Its Windows 98-style presentation is an inline, dismissible notice, not a blocking system error. Do not replace this supplied copy with BLANK or invent recruitment details/contact information.
- Never publish placeholder articles, fictional contributors, unpublished issues or events. Content collection entries remain unpublished until explicitly marked `approved: true` after owner review.
- Keep legal text separate from editorial prose. The operator explicitly approved public policies and removal of administrative launch blockers on 2026-09-26. Current public privacy/cookie notices and existing terms/disclaimer are approved for publication; private contributor-contract drafts are not executed agreements. Do not invent legal compliance claims, account verification or business details.
- Public policies should be concise and visitor-facing. The owner requested removal of individual/company-registration statements, internal implementation/audit notes, lists of nonexistent features and irrelevant professional-advice boilerplate. Keep factual data-use/provider/cookie/contact disclosures; do not claim that legally required identity information is universally unnecessary or invent personal details. No design or editorial changes are authorized by policy simplification.
- This policy persists until the owner explicitly changes it. No AI-generated editorial prose is permitted by default.

## Identity

- The owner expressly authorized public/llms.txt to publish their exact definition, Miami location and supplied current-mascot description. Preserve this wording; this narrow authorization does not permit generating other editorial copy or imply AI-training permission. The file is a public reference, not a guarantee of crawler ingestion or search placement.
- Glosso is an artistic and literary movement emphasizing oration, prosody and form in pursuit of the truest articulation of an experience. It rejects institutional and performative conventions in favor of the expressive possibilities of the individual's tongue.
- Glosso Collective is the founding organization, based in Miami and the Tri-State area. Glosso Magazine is its independent digital and forthcoming physical publication. This description is design context, not approved site copy.
- Do not prescribe a house aesthetic or writing style to contributors. Preserve the individuality of each work.
- The tongue is a recurring motif. Turquoise is the primary brand color.
- The frog artwork in `public/art/glosso-frog.png` is the creator's current original artwork. Preserve that file. Do not redraw, replace or alter it without explicit direction.
- Do not invent published issues, contributors, events, endorsements or contact details. Use honest forthcoming states.

## Development

- Astro 7 (owner-approved security upgrade), TypeScript, MDX, static output. Preserve the unified Markdown pipeline and `compressHTML: true` so the upgrade does not change editorial rendering. No database, visitor accounts, ads or paid services. The owner has now selected both Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare dashboard traffic analytics for the future launch. This does not approve an engagement metric or authorize activation/deployment; verify actual configuration before describing it as installed.
- Hosting is Cloudflare Workers Static Assets, not a new Pages project. Keep Astro static and the root `wrangler.jsonc`; no Cloudflare Astro adapter or Worker script is needed. Production build: `pnpm build:launch`; deploy: `npx wrangler deploy`; assets: `dist`; production branch: `main`. Use `pnpm build` for local verification only. Verify the guarded build in the actual Cloudflare account before pushing/merging to main; otherwise use a review PR. Preview deployments also require release safeguards and must not expose unreviewed material.
- `pnpm dev` starts a local preview at `http://127.0.0.1:4321/`.
- `pnpm preview` serves the production output after a build.
- New writing, issues and contributors go in `src/content/`. See `docs/PUBLISHING.md` for frontmatter and file examples.
- Contact and submission addresses are edited in `src/data/site.ts`. Keep them `null` until real addresses are supplied.
- The owner has supplied `contact@glosso.org` for general contact and the UX notice. Use that shared setting for both; do not assume it is also the submissions address. The UX notice's Email action opens a native mailto draft and reveals a readonly, selectable email field with a clipboard/manual-copy fallback. It does not submit a form or send an email automatically.
- Preserve punctuation, case, whitespace and line breaks in contributed work. Use `presentation: verbatim` with a `.md` raw-text body when exact spacing matters; ordinary JSX text inside an MDX pre can normalize indentation. Custom MDX remains supported.
- Keep navigation small, layouts readable and contributors' work visually generous. Avoid generic startup sections, grunge and excessive effects.
- The current visual direction is joyful, refined and slightly bizarre: saturated turquoise, vermilion ribbon forms, warm cream, curved geometry, expressive but restrained typography and generous editorial space. Art Deco informs proportion and rhythm, not decorative thin-line fans or repeated arches. Avoid scattered ornaments, gloomy backgrounds and unmotivated rules.
- Preserve the approved homepage hierarchy: current monthly issue first, latest article second, most-read work third, site/collective updates fourth. Do not change engagement measurement or the magazine's digital format without the owner's direction. The unchanged original frog is a small signature, not a dominant illustration. Keep contributor pages quiet and flexible. Use locally hosted, openly licensed fonts from public/fonts.
- Place the frog beside the Glosso name in the global home link. The visual system uses a layered folio-like issue display, an open latest-article layout, a smaller contrasting most-read panel and a sweeping squared ribbon. Use asymmetry, crisp overlapping planes and typographic contrast, not textures, fake shadows, invented covers or scattered ornaments. Keep artwork proportional and unmodified.
- The owner explicitly requested BLANK article layout placeholders. Render these only when no approved writing exists, visibly identify the section as an unpublished layout preview, and never create collection entries, dates, fake article links or published routes for them. All missing titles, descriptions and contributor names must remain exactly BLANK.
- Motion shares global duration/easing tokens: 650ms one-time entry/unfurl, 280ms spatial response, 180ms color response. Use the central Motion component; no continuous motion, pointer tracking or replay controls. Do not hide content behind animation; honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and no-JavaScript operation.
- Run `pnpm build` before sharing changes. Check a desktop and mobile viewport when changing layout.
- Never push or deploy without the owner's approval.
- Legal-review work must not redesign the site. New legal drafts stay in the gitignored `legal-review/` directory, outside Astro routes and public/. Preserve existing legal drafts as unreviewed until replacement is explicitly approved. Use conspicuous OPERATOR INPUT REQUIRED markers for unknown legal facts, rights and practices. Never mark launch approvals true without operator direction and appropriate legal review.
- Confirmed launch facts (2026-09-24): independently operated, no registered business, mailing list or monetization; submissions adults-only (18+ and legal capacity), international adult submissions welcome. No guardian intake workflow. Contributors retain copyright. Submission grants private receive/store/review permission only; publication requires a separate nonexclusive worldwide agreement covering website, digital PDF, agreed print issue, archive and reasonable approved promotion. Routine copyediting/layout is allowed; substantial changes need contributor approval. Glosso retains editorial discretion subject to those rights. Reprints, compensation, attribution and withdrawal must be explicit; special editions require creator and contributor permissions. Do not reopen these settled choices or infer AI-training permission. Proposed retention periods and withdrawal deadlines still need approval. See docs/FINAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md for the single current release checklist.
- Cloudflare's production build command must use `pnpm build:launch`. Required approvals are operatorApproved, publicPoliciesApproved and publicContentAndRightsReviewed; these now record the owner's explicit release authorization. legalReviewComplete and accountConfigurationVerified remain false/informational, not fabricated signoffs or mandatory gates. Private-output checks, remaining draft-marker checks and content/image validation still block. This supersedes older release-blocked status in audit records. Before any commit/push run `pnpm audit:repository` after explicit staging; never use blanket staging for drafts/private files. Ignore rules do not protect already tracked data or Git history.
- Latest legal clarification: the owner operates personally as an individual, with no mailing list, payments, advertising or subscriptions at launch. Editorial discretion includes scheduling, copyediting, presentation, headlines and removal, subject to contributor substantial-change approval and accurate attribution; this does not authorize AI-written headlines. WCAG 2.2 AA testing must distinguish automated/targeted results from full conformance and legal conclusions. Forms and actual public PDFs must be tested if present, never reported as passed when absent. The dev-only axe-core checker is not a website analytics script.

## Publishing infrastructure and design freeze

- The visual design is approved. Publishing work must not redesign it or change motion, layout, colors, type or artwork.
- The owner subsequently requested substantial visual renovation using the supplied sculptural references, explicitly rejecting the previous minimal polish. Preserve palette, hierarchy, publishing architecture and original frog; reference images are inspiration, not publication assets. Use sculptural curved stages, overlapping paper planes and clear editorial contrast.
- The September 24 visual refinement uses Miami's projecting canopies and FIU's intersecting sculptural planes as abstract references, not copied artwork. Reuse PublicationStage for the issue surround and page-intro geometry; retain the clean, uncropped PublicationImage inside it. Rounded returns, crisp cream reveals, black structural edges and orange accents form one system. Keep this visual language around the publication, not imposed on contributors' work. Do not translate design-context definitions into public copy.
- The subsequent cover-proportion revision replaces the folio's pedestal with an open, asymmetric aperture: two counter-curves suggest articulation and leave room for the tongue to continue outward. Keep the cover itself proportional, gently tilted and independent of this decoration. The compact ribbon must turn into its gutter before the issue copy begins; measure that clearance rather than assuming a fixed bend depth.
- The homepage tongue must be a single connected path anchored beneath the folio and into the Most Read panel. PublicationRibbon measures real layout anchors on resize/content changes; do not replace it with disconnected pseudo-element bookmarks. Keep decoration away from copy and images, and respect reduced motion. No-JavaScript use must retain every publication/navigation function.
- When adjusting this composition, run `node scripts/check-ribbon.mjs` against the local preview with `GLOSSO_PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` and optionally `GLOSSO_CHROME_PATH` pointing to an installed browser runtime. `GLOSSO_QA_DIR` enables screenshots. The check tests six widths, exact endpoints, text clearance and layout changes. Do not add test publications to live collections.
- Use PublicationImage for browsing frames. Issue `cover` fills the homepage folio and archive; writing `thumbnail` optionally overrides `cover` on browsing cards only. Default to contain, never crop artwork automatically. Explicit thumbnail crop/focal-point settings must not affect the reading-page original. Include thumbnail sources in managed-media release checks.
- Preserve supplied image alternatives. Set coverAlt, thumbnail.alt and portraitAlt in the existing content metadata; see docs/IMAGE_ALT_TEXT.md. Meaningful released images need accurate alternatives, not BLANK or invented artwork descriptions. Explicit empty alt is only for decorative/redundant images, including the header frog beside its named home link. The build checks rendered HTML images, including Markdown/MDX body images; run pnpm test:images. This technical check is not a full accessibility certification.
- Reuse the four existing Astro collections and shared routes. Monthly and special editions both use `/issues/<stable-slug>/`; `edition: special` never enters the homepage monthly slot.
- All routes, indexes, sitemap and managed media use `publishedContent()`. Release requires `approved: true`, `draft: false`, supplied required editorial fields and a reached publication timestamp. BLANK drafts must not gain public routes.
- Every released work requires approved contributor credits. Reject broken references, missing local media and duplicate monthly issues at build time.
- Managed media belongs in `publication-assets/`, referenced as `/media/...`; only released references are emitted. Anything in `public/` is always public. Never put private submissions or secrets in public/, collection files, or a public Git repository.
- Scheduled publishing is build-time selection, not a browser timer or automatic deployment. A new build/deployment is necessary at or after release; deployment still requires approval.
- No engagement definition has been approved. Keep `engagementApproved` false and the homepage ranking unpopulated until the owner supplies and approves the metric, source, window, ties and duplicate handling. The legacy pageview helper is not permission to choose that metric.
- `pnpm new:content` creates only BLANK, unapproved drafts and refuses overwrites. Run `pnpm test:publishing`, `pnpm test:homepage` and `pnpm build`. Never write test publications to the live collections or production output.
